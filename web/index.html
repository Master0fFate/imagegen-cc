Use an online image service

Any service with an OpenAI-compatible images endpoint works. Enter its base address, your key, and the model name from the service's documentation.

The key goes to your operating system's password store. It never appears in Claude's conversation.

Use your own ComfyUI

Already running ComfyUI with a fast setup? Connect it and Claude uses it at full speed. Load the model lists, pick Qwen-Image 2.1 files, and save. Or point to your own workflow exported with Workflow, Export (API), with {{prompt}} where the text goes.

Your backends

Claude uses the default backend unless you name another one. Test makes one small real image so you can see that everything works. With an online service a test can cost money.
